# Film production conventions

For every newly written video prompt, regardless of project, characters, location,
genre, visual style or aspect ratio, follow `docs/CLIP_PROMPT_STANDARD.md`.
The owner made the cinematic format the shared standard on 2026-10-01 after
approving the ten-clip test. It applies to manual authoring and Automation's
single-clip and batch writers. Use GPT through the existing Avis provider.

Derive content from the current shotlist, approved character/environment/prop/crowd
profiles, supplied images, timing and scene continuity. Samples teach format only;
never import their characters, wardrobe, setting, actions or end states. Keep exact
dialogue and its language, speaker and delivery. Preserve known source facts and
log compatible staging choices separately. Missing required data must be reported,
not invented or replaced by the old template.

Preserve existing approved prompts unless rewriting is requested or their inputs
change under the normal validation rules. Image-sheet prompts retain their own
approved sheet format; the video timeline format is not an image-sheet format.
Do not reintroduce an application path that silently creates legacy video prompts.

For every new video prompt, explicitly include in AUDIO:
`NO BACKGROUND MUSIC. NO BGM. NO SCORE.` Keep supplied dialogue, room tone and
motivated sound effects; do not recreate source-video music. This applies to every
film style and project (owner reaffirmed on 2026-10-07).
