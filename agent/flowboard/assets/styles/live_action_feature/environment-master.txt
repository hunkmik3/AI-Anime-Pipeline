INPUT HIERARCHY
The ENVIRONMENT PROFILE is the absolute source of truth for location identity, story period, interior/exterior, architecture, layout, scale, circulation, entrance/exit relationships, windows, furniture zones, materials, actual time of day, weather, story function and continuity locks.
The SELECTED STYLE changes visual representation, drawing/rendering, material treatment and lighting interpretation only. It must not relocate the story, substitute a style-associated country or time period, add magical architecture or convert a school into a palace.
Use the requested view/state in the profile; otherwise create an establishing wide that clearly communicates the supplied space. No image references are supplied. Do not invent a binding to an image.

CORE GOAL
Create a reusable film location with understandable geography and action space, not merely a decorative picture. Follow specified spatial anchors exactly. Where dimensions or counts are unspecified, make a restrained coherent visual proposal without labelling it as a source fact. Repeat visible architectural modules consistently; do not create impossible openings or passages.

SELECTED STYLE
Hollywood live-action feature-film cinematography; photorealistic imagery with an 8K Ultra HD level of fine detail. Real human anatomy, distinctive faces, natural pores and fine lines, subtle skin colour variation, individual hair strands and believable eyes. Preserve each character’s specified age, identity and proportions. Render fabric weave, leather grain, metal wear, wood fibres and stone with physically believable texture and reflections. Maintain this photographic visual language across all shots and asset references. Avoid waxy skin, beauty-filter smoothing, artificial sharpening, glossy game-like surfaces and digital-painting textures.

Environments should feel like tangible locations and constructed film sets, with coherent scale, practical construction, natural imperfections and believable contact between objects. Follow the project’s established period, architecture, costume and prop rules. Atmospheric and magical effects, when present, should have a clear source, restrained intensity and convincing interaction with nearby surfaces.

For narrative scenes, use motivated daylight, moonlight, windows, practical fixtures or fire appropriate to the setting. Keep faces readable, highlights gently rolled off and shadows detailed; preserve natural skin tones within the scene’s colour palette. Use realistic lens perspective and selective depth of field for emotion, with sufficient depth for geography and important actions. For white-background asset references, use neutral studio lighting, accurate colours and even focus across all required details and views.

ABSOLUTE QUALITY FLOOR
Even simple or worn locations deserve coherent perspective, clear shapes, controlled composition and carefully executed light. Do not turn ordinary settings into luxury environments. Use material rendering appropriate to the chosen medium, preserving the profile's actual colours and material identities. In 2D, suggest surface detail through purposeful paint/line/shadow design, not photographic texture pasted onto a drawing.

TIME / LIGHTING / MOOD
Actual time of day, weather and light-source locations come from the profile. Apply the selected style's cinematic quality without changing day to night or adding unsupported fog, fire, magic or coloured effects. Keep important action areas and face-height illumination readable, with a coherent light direction and sensible contact shadows. Environmental objects should be grounded and belong to the same visual medium as future characters.

PRODUCTION GEOGRAPHY
Clearly communicate the entrance, exits, circulation direction, key action zone, staging space and stable spatial anchors as specified. Preserve wall-side relationships, door/locker rhythm, floor continuity and plausible human scale. Leave usable space for actors and the camera. The view must support later wides, medium shots, OTS, reverses and inserts in the same setting.

CAMERA / OUTPUT
One single 16:9 landscape environment plate. Full-frame establishing wide, eye-level, level horizon, coherent approximately 28mm-equivalent perspective and believable verticals unless the profile explicitly specifies another view. Readable foreground, middle ground and background, with useful focus through staging areas. No collage, multipanel sheet, floorplan inset, captions, logo or watermark.

POPULATION
For this reusable asset plate, show the clean unoccupied setting unless the profile explicitly requests a populated plate. Do not add hero characters, human silhouettes, character turnarounds or invented animals. An unoccupied location reference never means the story crowd has exited; population is assembled later using the shotlist and cast.

ARTIFACT / CONTINUITY CHECK
Avoid warped walls, conflicting vanishing points, implausible duplicated openings, impossible stairs, floating objects, melted furniture, broken reflections, irrational shadows, pattern glitches, random text, oversharpening and fake HDR. Preserve architecture, geography, materials and lighting relationships under the selected visual style.
